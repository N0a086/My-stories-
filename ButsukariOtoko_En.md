「ぶつかりおじさんの生息地」

**The Habitat of the Shoulder-Checker**

On the way home from school, right there in the station, I saw one in the flesh — a Shoulder-Checker, one of those middle-aged men you hear about, the ones who slam into people in a crowd and keep walking. He landed a textbook hit on a woman who was just walking along, then made off at a brisk, fleeing sort of walk.

*"Guess I'll follow him."*

Don't underestimate the boredom of a high school boy. That was roughly my thinking as I set off after him.

I wanted to see what kind of miserable little life he went home to.

He rode the train and got off about twenty minutes past my own stop. The crowd was thicker than I'd expected, and I nearly lost him.

*"Ow."*

Something took my shoulder, hard. A different Shoulder-Checker this time.

Thud. Crack. Thud. And it wasn't just me. All around me, Shoulder-Checkers were colliding with one another.

From here to the ticket gates is a two-minute walk. With the bumping, though, longer than that.

I tried to keep after the first man, but I kept getting hit and couldn't make any real progress.

I already wanted to apologize to him.

*"This is a battlefield."*
